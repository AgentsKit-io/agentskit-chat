feat(chat): ship drizzle-kit migrations for the session and decision tables

`@agentskit/chat/drizzle-pg` now ships drizzle-kit migrations and `migrateChatSchema(db)`, which applies them with their own journal table (`chatMigrationsTable`). Databases created with the 0.7.0 DDL are adopted without manual SQL or data loss. `chatSessionTable` and `chatDecisionTable` now declare the DDL's CHECK and primary-key constraints, under the names Postgres gave them, so a host's own drizzle-kit sees no drift. ADR-0040.
