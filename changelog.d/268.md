test: RF-06 turn-server contract on Node and workerd in CI

A new CI job, `runtime-contract`, runs one complete metered turn through `createChatHandler` against PostgreSQL 16 on a Node `Pool` and in local workerd. The turn uses Drizzle sessions and decisions, upstream `postgresChatMemory`, and upstream `postgresCostStore` with a cost ceiling. Run it locally with `pnpm test:runtime:pg`.
